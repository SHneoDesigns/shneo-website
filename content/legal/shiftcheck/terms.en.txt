## 2. Description of the service

ShiftCheck helps you plan and document your shifts for your own use: with shift types, rotations and a manual plan, planned and actual working times with breaks, absences (vacation, sick, other), manually marked holidays, notes, premium rules you define yourself (for example night, Sunday, holiday, overtime or custom rules), an optional hourly wage, a monthly evaluation and a payroll check against values from your own payslip. Local reminders, backups and a CSV export of a month complement the app. Your entries are stored locally on your device. No account or registration with SHneoDesigns is required.

## 3. Calculations, payroll check and no advice

ShiftCheck calculates working times, premiums and monthly evaluations solely on the basis of your own entries and the rules you define yourself. The results are estimates. ShiftCheck does not know your employment contract or collective agreement or the rules that apply to you, and takes them into account only as far as you enter them yourself as a rule. The app does not calculate net pay, taxes or social security contributions.

For the payroll check, you type in values from your own payslip, and ShiftCheck shows you differences to your own records ("Check deviation"). A deviation shown is only a hint to check the information concerned. It is not a statement that your employer made a mistake; it can also result, for example, from an incomplete or incorrect entry, a different rule or rounding.

ShiftCheck does not provide, and does not replace, legal, tax or employment law advice. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of your entries and rules. If you have questions about your pay or your entitlements, please contact your employer or a qualified adviser. Liability under section 12 remains unaffected.
